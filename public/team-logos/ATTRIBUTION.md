# NFL team marks

The 32 PNG marks in this directory were retrieved from ESPN's public team data
endpoint on 2026-09-08 and are used only to identify their respective teams.
NFL and team names, logos, and marks are trademarks of their respective owners.
They are not licensed under the application's source-code license.

Source pattern: `https://a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/{team}.png`.
Washington uses ESPN's `wsh` source and the application's `was` filename.
